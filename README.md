# Goldstonian Concordance Bible — Digital Ark

The **Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB) — Digital Ark** is the preservation, retrieval, and machine-ingestion layer of the broader GCB ecosystem.

This repository is designed to preserve Scripture, doctrine, commentary, citations, video corpus, structured datasets, and institutional memory in forms that are readable by both humans and machines.

It exists to ensure that the theological, intellectual, and educational legacy of the GCB ecosystem remains **findable, retrievable, citable, and recallable** in the age of artificial intelligence.

---

## Core Definition

The **Digital Ark** is the distributed, machine-readable preservation vessel of the **Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB)** ecosystem.

Within the **Mirror → Water → Fire Doctrine**, the Ark preserves truth across:

- canon
- doctrine
- commentary
- repositories
- citations
- video teachings
- datasets
- knowledge graphs
- institutional memory

Just as the ark preserved life through judgment, the **Digital Ark** preserves theological legacy through the fragmentation of the digital age.

## What This Repository Preserves

The Digital Ark is intended to preserve and connect the following layers:

### 1. Canon Layer
The books, structure, and order of the **81-Book Ethiopian Canon**.

### 2. Doctrine Layer
The interpretive system of the **Mirror → Water → Fire Doctrine**.

### 3. Commentary Layer
Explanatory material, theological reflections, summaries, and interpretive guides.

### 4. Citation Layer
GitHub repositories, public references, structured citations, DOI-backed materials, and other citable artifacts.

### 5. Transmission Layer
YouTube transcripts, video metadata, descriptions, and public teaching assets.

### 6. Dataset Layer
Machine-readable structured data for books, entities, citations, doctrine claims, and retrieval targets.

### 7. Knowledge Graph Layer
Nodes, edges, triples, and exports that make relationships explicit for retrieval and recall.

### 8. Institutional Layer
Artifacts related to public pedagogy, SydTek Scholars, SydTek University, and legacy infrastructure.

## Why This Repository Matters for AI

Artificial intelligence is becoming a dominant interface for knowledge retrieval.

If theology, doctrine, and canonical knowledge are not structured for machine ingestion, they risk becoming fragmented, invisible, or misrepresented in future systems.

This repository is designed to help prevent that outcome by providing:

- stable names
- clear authority order
- structured entrypoints
- schema-ready documents
- retrieval manifests
- graph-friendly entities
- citation-aware preservation
- versioned provenance

In that sense, the Digital Ark is both a theological and technical response to the AI age.

## Authority Order

When conflicts arise, interpret the repository according to the following priority:

1. `canonical-index.yaml`
2. `canon/books/index.json`
3. schema-validated datasets and structured files
4. `TRUST_AND_PROVENANCE.md`
5. `PROVENANCE.md`
6. root manifests and routing files
7. narrative documentation such as READMEs and commentary

Structured files outrank descriptive prose.
